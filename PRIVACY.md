# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Netflix Ratings has no server, user accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. The developer does not receive any of your data.

## What is sent to other sites

To find scores, the extension makes these requests directly from your browser:

- **IMDb** (`v3.sg.media-imdb.com`, `datasets.imdbws.com`): the names of titles displayed on Netflix pages you visit, and downloads of IMDb's public ratings and episode files.
- **Rotten Tomatoes** (`www.rottentomatoes.com`): the names of titles displayed on Netflix pages you visit.
- **Netflix** (`www.netflix.com`): requests for public title pages, to confirm a title's name, type and year.

These sites receive the requests under their own privacy policies and can see normal request information such as your IP address.

## What is stored

- Your minimum scores and display preferences are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`, which your browser may sync to your browser account.
- IMDb scores and cached lookup results are saved locally in your browser. They are removed when you uninstall the extension.

The extension does not read or store your Netflix password, payment details, profile information or viewing history. It reads only the titles displayed on Netflix pages.

## Data sharing

Your data is not sold or used for advertising or credit decisions. Nothing is shared with anyone apart from the requests described above.
